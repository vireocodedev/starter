# Phase 5 — flagship experience and public beta

Phase 5 turns the technically complete golden path into a credible, observable
evaluation experience. The flagship remains one production-shaped Item vertical
slice; it may deepen that workflow and its presentation, but it must not imply
support for additional domain models that are not generated, tested, and owned.

Engineering completion and independent adoption are separate. A maintainer-owned
deployment, synthetic monitor, automated clean room, download, star, or issue does
not count as an independent team actively building with Vireo.

## Evidence classes

- **Required automation:** deterministic accessibility, responsive, build,
  deployment, reset, link, and proof-surface checks.
- **Hosted operations evidence:** public deployment, scheduled health/journey
  results, incident history, and retained reset evidence tied to a revision.
- **External workflow evidence:** privacy-reviewed aggregate results from people
  unaffiliated with implementation.
- **Independent adoption evidence:** a real team controls its application and
  deployment, makes application-owned changes, and attempts a supported upgrade.
- **Explicit limitation:** a public statement that prevents a demo or synthetic
  result from becoming a broader product, uptime, demand, or production claim.

## Non-goals

- Adding another flagship domain solely to make the demo appear larger.
- Representing engine-level browser coverage as branded-device evidence.
- Collecting telemetry, participant identity, or application data by default.
- Promising an uptime SLA before an owned host and response rotation exist.
- Treating automated evaluation, stars, downloads, or maintainer activity as an
  independent adopter.

## Gate

Phase 5 closes only when `P5-07` passes: three independent teams are actively
building with Vireo and at least one has deployed and maintained it. Engineering
may be complete while this gate remains open. Phase 6 launch planning may be
prepared, but a 1.0 commitment must also satisfy the remaining Phase 4 independent
and manual evidence.
